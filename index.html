AEDP-Informed EFCT Consultation Group

This is a monthly supervision group with Anne Crile, LMFT and John Wiskind, LCSW, MPH. Both of us are ICCEFT certified EFT supervisors. Anne is a level 3 AEDP therapist and John is a certified AEDP supervisor. This group counts toward hours for EFT certification when presenting.

The group integrates tape review, didactic discussions, experiential learning, and conceptual elements of both AEDP and EFT.

Both of us identify as white. We have been, and continue to, explore and learn around issues of race, racism and white supremacy. It's important to us to be direct about our intention to infuse our work and this supervision group with this framework in mind. We're looking for the group to act as a brave space to explore the ways that these issues emerge both systemically and in internalized ways within us and within the couples we’re treating.

We are excited to help you find and refine your integrated authentic AEDP/EFT voice. This group came about, in large part, due to our awareness of how our EFT work was enriched and deepened as a result of our AEDP training. As we talked with more colleagues, we realized there was a real thirst for ways to explicitly integrate these two complimentary modalities.

This group is open to ICEEFT Core Skills participants/graduates who also have AEDP training (Level 1/Immersion, or equivalent online training). Please be currently recording or open to recording your couples sessions. We are eager to help you begin recording if that's a hurdle you're struggling to overcome.

Tuition for the series is $600 and the group is capped at 8 participants. We want the group to be accessible. If you're from an under-represented group and the tuition is a hinderance, please reach out to us.

The group will meet monthly on Fridays for two hours from 10:00 AM to 12:00 Pacific on the following dates:

Each participant will have the opportunity to present once, with two participants sharing their work each meeting.

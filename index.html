AEDP Informed EFCT Consultation Group

This will be a monthly supervision group with Anne Crile, LMFT and John Wiskind, LCSW. Both of us are ICCEFT certified EFT supervisors. Anne is a level 3 AEDP therapist and John is a certified AEDP supervisor. This group can count toward hours for EFT certification when presenting.

The group will be a mix of didactic, experiential, tape review and conceptual using the ACES/HEARTS model.

Both of us identify as white and we have both been doing a lot of affirmative exploration around issues of race, racism and white supremacy. It is important to us that the group is a brave space to explore the ways that these issues emerge both systemically and in internalized ways in us in and in the couples we’re working with.

This group is open to ICEEFT Core Skills participants/graduates who also have AEDP training (Level 1/Immersion, or equivalent online training). Please be currently recording or open to recording your couples sessions.

Tuition for the series is $400.

The group will meet monthly on Fridays for two hours from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PST on the following dates:

  • September 12, 2025
  • October 10, 2025
  • November 14, 2025
  • December 12, 2025

Each participant will have the opportunity to present once, with two participants sharing their work each meeting.

Please reach out to either one of us with questions and/or to confirm your participation.
